About Zuboy

Zuboy (Hassan Nura) is a Nigerian content creator, digital entertainer and music artist publicly known by the name Zuboy. He creates engaging and relatable content across social media, combining comedy, storytelling, lifestyle and entertainment.

His audience also knows him through “Na Hauwa,” a name associated with his recurring Hauwa-themed content.

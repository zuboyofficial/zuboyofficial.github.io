ABOUT ZUBOY

A creator built around connection, creativity and entertainment.

Zuboy (Hassan Nura) is a Nigerian content creator, digital entertainer and music artist publicly known by the name Zuboy. His work combines comedy, storytelling, lifestyle, relationship-focused content and entertainment in a relatable social-media style.

He is also associated with the name “Na Hauwa” through recurring Hauwa-themed content that has become part of his creator identity.
